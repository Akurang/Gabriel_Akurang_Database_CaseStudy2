A materialized view store the view as a temporary table in the database when the view is first queried.
The base table Part is not needed when the effect of the update does not satisfy the condition in the view.
For instance, partcost<= 1000 inserted, deleted or updated will not make any changes to the view.
A challenge occurs when partCost >= 1000 or a modification of partNNo is made due to the distinct key word.
